HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap your own slice of the sky to shoot. You can only hit ducks flying inside your slice.
  • On a keyboard each kid has one key — the key shoots at the duck nearest the middle of your slice.
  • 🦆 big duck 1 point · 🐤 small fast duck 2 points · 🥇 golden duck 5 points.
  • Watch out — dark birds with a red chest are not ducks. Shoot one and you lose 3 points. Let them fly past!
  • Hit ducks in a row and your points multiply, up to ×3. One miss and the streak is gone.
  • Six rounds, six different skies. The dog laughs at every duck that gets away!
