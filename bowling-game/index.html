STRIKE PARTY

1 to 4 kids bowl on one screen, each on their own lane. The ball, the pins and the lane all use real bowling numbers - a 7,26 kg ball against 1,53 kg pins - so a good hit into the pocket topples the whole rack the way it does at a real alley. Scoring is the real 10-pin scoring, strikes and spares included.
