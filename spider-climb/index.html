HOW TO PLAY

  • You climb by yourself while you hang on a wall. That never stops.
  • Tap anywhere to leap across to the other tower. Time it before the wall ahead is blocked.
  • Hold the CLIMB button to scramble up faster. You cover more ground, but you get less time to read what is coming.
  • Falling? Tap again to shoot a web line and catch the wall - each catch costs one web shot. Fall off the bottom and it costs a life.
  • Tap the WEB button to shoot down drones, birds and loose gear. The dashed ring shows what you will hit.
  • Glass makes you slide, cracked panels break if you hang on, live cables shock - but your web can cut them.
  • Coins sit on the risky side. Land a clean jump to keep your combo and your score multiplier climbing.
  • Keyboard: up arrow to climb fast, left and right to leap to that tower, space to leap across, F to shoot web, P to pause.
