8-BALL POOL — DUEL

Two kids take turns on the same device. The first to clear their own group and pot the 8 ball wins!

1 Drag back from the cue ball (like a slingshot) to aim and charge
2 Release to shoot — the further you drag, the harder the hit
3 Tap the large cue ball below the table to pick one of 9 contact points — top, bottom or side spin
4 Pot one of your own balls and you shoot again
5 Keys ← → fine-tune the angle, Space to shoot
📖

RULES

🎱 the 9 cue ball contact points: Tap the large cue ball below the table to choose where the tip strikes the cue ball — TOP spin sends the cue after the object ball, BOTTOM spin draws the cue back, LEFT/RIGHT english kicks the cue off the cushion to that side. It resets to centre after each shot.
🎯 Choosing your group: After the break, whoever pots first takes that group — solids 1-7 or stripes 9-15.
🔁 Shooting again: Pot a ball from your own group and you get another turn.
⚠️ Fouls: Potting the cue ball, hitting nothing, or hitting the opponent's ball first. Your opponent then gets ball in hand.
🏆 Winning: Clear your whole group, then pot the 8 ball in a pocket.
💀 Thua ngay: Potting the 8 before clearing your group, or potting the cue ball together with the 8, loses the frame.
