How to colour

  • 1. Tap a colour at the bottom.
  • 2. Tap any part of the picture to fill it.
  • 3. Tap it again with another colour to change it.
  • 4. With stickers on, drag a sticker to move it, or tap it to resize or take it off.

Nothing can go wrong here - there is no timer, no score and no way to lose. Every picture is saved by itself, so your little one can come back to it later.
