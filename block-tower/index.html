BLOCK TOWER

A well of real 3D blocks floating in mid-air. Stack brick, stone, wood and metal - and grab the gold blocks, they are worth triple.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the left or right side of the screen to slide the block. Tap the middle to turn it.
  • Swipe down - or press the big button - to drop it straight away.
  • On a keyboard: arrow keys to move and turn, space to drop, P to pause.
  • Fill a whole row and it bursts. Four rows at once is worth far more than one at a time.
  • 🥇 Gold blocks are worth triple. A row made only of gold is the best you can do.
  • The pale outline shows exactly where the block will land - aim with it.
