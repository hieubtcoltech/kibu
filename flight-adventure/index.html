HOW TO FLY

  • 🚀 Speed up to roll down the runway, then ▲ pull up to fly.
  • ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys fly up, down, left and right.
  • Fly through the glowing rings and collect stars.
  • When a famous place appears, press 📷 to take a photo.
  • Near the end, press 🛬 and the game helps you land.
  • Press 🪄 any time and the plane flies by itself.
  • You can never crash. If you fly too low, the game lifts you up.
