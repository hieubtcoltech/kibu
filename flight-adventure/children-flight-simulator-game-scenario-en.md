# Flight Adventure Kids: Game Scenario Document

## 1. Game Vision

### Working Title
Flight Adventure Kids

### Game Idea
Flight Adventure Kids is a gentle flying adventure for children. The player controls a small friendly airplane, takes off from a real-world inspired airport, flies across beautiful skies and landscapes, sees famous cities and landmarks, then lands safely at a nearby airport.

The game is not meant to be a serious flight simulator. It should feel simple, magical, relaxing, and easy for children to enjoy.

### Core Experience
The child should feel:

- "I am flying the airplane myself."
- "I can look around and enjoy the sky."
- "I can visit real places around the world."
- "Landing is exciting, but not scary."
- "Every route shows me something new."

### Main Promise
Pick a place, take off, fly through the sky, discover beautiful sights, and land safely.

## 2. Target Audience

### Main Audience
Children around 5-10 years old.

### Design Tone
The experience should be:

- Safe.
- Calm.
- Colorful.
- Encouraging.
- Easy to understand.
- Focused on exploration, not failure.

### What the Game Should Avoid

- Combat.
- Crashes shown in a scary way.
- Complicated airplane instruments.
- Punishing failure.
- Long reading.
- Stressful timers.
- Hard landing requirements.

## 3. Core Gameplay Loop

1. The child chooses a flight route.
2. The airplane appears on the runway.
3. The child speeds up and takes off.
4. The airplane rises into the sky.
5. The child gently steers through clouds, over cities, mountains, rivers, forests, and oceans.
6. Famous landmarks appear along the route.
7. The child can take photos of special sights.
8. The game suggests a nearby airport when the journey is almost complete.
9. The child follows simple landing guidance.
10. The airplane lands safely.
11. The child receives travel stamps, photos, and route progress.
12. The child can choose another destination.

## 4. Player Fantasy

The player is a young pilot on a sightseeing adventure.

The fantasy is not about mastering difficult aviation rules. It is about the joy of flying:

- Taking off from a runway.
- Seeing clouds pass by.
- Flying above rivers and cities.
- Spotting famous landmarks.
- Watching sunrise or sunset from the sky.
- Landing at a real airport.
- Filling a travel album with memories.

## 5. Game Modes

| Mode | Description | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Guided Flight | A complete route from one airport to another | Main game mode |
| Free Flight | Fly around a selected region and land when ready | Relaxed exploration |
| Landmark Tour | A short flight focused on famous sights | Quick sightseeing |
| Sunset Flight | A calm scenic flight during sunset | Visual reward |
| First Flight | A very easy tutorial route | For young children |

## 6. Flight Route Structure

Each route should feel like a small journey with a beginning, middle, and ending.

### Route Flow

| Phase | What Happens | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Airport Start | Plane waits on the runway | Anticipation |
| Takeoff | Plane speeds up and lifts into the sky | Excitement |
| Departure City | Player sees the city near the airport | Discovery |
| Nature Segment | Mountains, forests, rivers, clouds, or ocean | Relaxation |
| Landmark Moment | A famous place appears | Wonder |
| Cruise Segment | The plane flies smoothly through the sky | Freedom |
| Destination Approach | The next city or airport appears | Focus |
| Landing | Plane gently touches down | Achievement |
| Travel Summary | Photos and stamps are shown | Reward |

## 7. Suggested Starting Routes

### Route 1: Hanoi to Da Nang

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| Starting Airport | Noi Bai Airport |
| Destination Airport | Da Nang Airport |
| Mood | First adventure, bright and friendly |
| Main Scenery | City, green fields, mountains, coastline |
| Famous Sights | Hoan Kiem Lake, limestone mountains, Hai Van Pass, Dragon Bridge, My Khe Beach |
| Best Time of Day | Morning or sunset |
| Difficulty | Very easy |

#### Scenario
The child takes off from Hanoi in the morning. The city slowly becomes smaller below. The plane flies over green fields and winding rivers. Far ahead, soft mountains appear through the clouds. Later, the sea comes into view, and the coastline of Da Nang shines in the sun. The Dragon Bridge appears near the city. The child takes a photo, then follows the runway lights and lands safely in Da Nang.

### Route 2: Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| Starting Airport | Noi Bai Airport |
| Destination Airport | Tan Son Nhat Airport |
| Mood | Longer domestic journey |
| Main Scenery | Hanoi city, central coastline, clouds, southern city skyline |
| Famous Sights | Hoan Kiem Lake, central beaches, Saigon River, Landmark 81 |
| Best Time of Day | Daytime to golden afternoon |
| Difficulty | Easy |

#### Scenario
The journey begins in Hanoi. After takeoff, the plane climbs through white clouds and follows a long route toward the south. The child sees rivers, mountains, beaches, and small towns below. Near the end of the flight, the skyline of Ho Chi Minh City appears. The Saigon River curves through the city, and Landmark 81 rises in the distance. The plane turns gently toward Tan Son Nhat Airport and lands.

### Route 3: Ho Chi Minh City to Singapore

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| Starting Airport | Tan Son Nhat Airport |
| Destination Airport | Changi Airport |
| Mood | First international trip |
| Main Scenery | City, river, ocean, islands, modern skyline |
| Famous Sights | Saigon River, ocean route, Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay |
| Best Time of Day | Afternoon or evening |
| Difficulty | Easy-medium |

#### Scenario
The child takes off from Ho Chi Minh City and flies above the Saigon River. The land slowly changes into open sea. Small islands and boats appear below. The sky turns golden as Singapore comes closer. The child sees Marina Bay Sands and the glowing city skyline. Changi Airport appears ahead, and the plane lands smoothly.

### Route 4: Da Nang Free Flight

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| Starting Airport | Da Nang Airport |
| Destination Airport | Player choice |
| Mood | Relaxed sightseeing |
| Main Scenery | Beach, mountains, bridges, city |
| Famous Sights | Dragon Bridge, Son Tra Peninsula, My Khe Beach |
| Best Time of Day | Sunset |
| Difficulty | Very easy |

#### Scenario
The child flies around Da Nang without a strict destination. The plane can turn toward the beach, circle near the Dragon Bridge, fly close to green mountains, or follow the coastline. When the child is ready, the game suggests the nearest airport. Landing is simple and guided.

### Route 5: Vietnam to Japan

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| Starting Airport | Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City |
| Destination Airport | Tokyo |

| Main Scenery | Clouds, ocean, islands, large city |

#### Scenario
This route feels like a bigger trip. The child flies above clouds and ocean, then sees Japan appear in the distance. Mount Fuji rises above the clouds as a special photo moment. Near Tokyo, the city becomes bright and busy. Tokyo Tower and Skytree appear before the plane approaches the destination airport.

### Route 6: Vietnam to the United States

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| Starting Airport | Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City |
| Destination Airport | Los Angeles |
| Mood | Grand adventure |
| Main Scenery | Ocean, clouds, sunrise, American coastline |
| Famous Sights | Pacific Ocean, Golden Gate Bridge optional scene, Los Angeles skyline |

#### Scenario
This should feel like a special long-distance journey. The flight is divided into short scenic chapters. The child sees the sun change position, flies above deep blue ocean, passes through soft clouds, and eventually reaches the American coast. The final part shows a large city skyline before landing in Los Angeles.

## 8. Airport Selection

### Airport Role
Airports are the start and end points of each adventure. They should feel friendly and easy to recognize.

### Airport Screen
Each airport card should show:

- Airport name.
- City.
- Country.
- A small illustration.
- Available routes.
- Difficulty level.
- Weather or time-of-day options.

### Suggested Airports

| Airport | City | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Noi Bai | Hanoi | Main starting airport |
| Tan Son Nhat | Ho Chi Minh City | Domestic and international hub |
| Da Nang | Da Nang | Scenic beach and mountain flights |
| Changi | Singapore | First international airport |
| Tokyo Airport | Tokyo | Medium international destination |
| Los Angeles Airport | Los Angeles | Long-distance special destination |

### Nearby Airport Landing
During Free Flight, the child can choose to land at the nearest airport.

The game should say something simple like:

"There is an airport nearby. Would you like to land?"

If the child says yes, the plane gently turns toward the airport and shows an easy landing path.

## 9. World and Scenery Design

### Overall World Feeling
The world should feel colorful, soft, and readable. It does not need to be realistic in every detail. It should suggest real places using clear shapes, colors, and famous landmarks.

### Scenery Types

| Scenery | Visual Ideas | Use |
|---|---|---|
| City | Tall buildings, roads, rivers, bridges | Near airports |
| Countryside | Green fields, small houses, rivers | Domestic flights |
| Mountains | Layered hills, forests, mist | Scenic route sections |
| Ocean | Blue water, islands, boats, sunlight | International and coastal flights |
| Clouds | Soft white clouds at different distances | All routes |
| Sunset | Orange sky, glowing clouds | Reward flights |
| Night City | City lights, stars, moon | Later unlock |

### Scenery Rhythm
The flight should never feel empty for too long.

Every route should include:

- A calm takeoff view.
- A city view.
- A natural landscape.
- At least one landmark moment.
- A beautiful sky moment.
- A clear landing approach.

## 10. Famous Landmarks

Landmarks are special moments during the flight. They are not obstacles. They are rewards for looking around.

### Landmark Behavior

- The landmark appears slowly in the distance.
- A small label shows the name.
- A camera button becomes active.
- The child can take a photo.
- The photo is added to the travel album.
- A short fact may appear or be spoken.

### Landmark Examples

| Location | Landmark | Short Child-Friendly Fact |

| Hanoi | Hoan Kiem Lake | A famous lake in the center of Hanoi. |
| Da Nang | Dragon Bridge | A bridge shaped like a dragon. |
| Da Nang | My Khe Beach | A long beach beside the city. |
| Ho Chi Minh City | Landmark 81 | One of the tallest buildings in Vietnam. |
| Singapore | Marina Bay Sands | A famous building near the bay. |
| Singapore | Gardens by the Bay | A garden with giant tree-like towers. |
| Japan | Mount Fuji | A famous mountain in Japan. |
| Tokyo | Tokyo Tower | A bright tower in Tokyo. |
| United States | Golden Gate Bridge | A famous red bridge by the sea. |
| United States | Los Angeles Skyline | A large city near the Pacific Ocean. |

Environmental moments make the sky feel alive.

### Sky Moments

| Moment | Description |
|---|---|
| Sunrise | The sun slowly rises with soft yellow light |
| Clear Day | Blue sky and white clouds |
| Golden Afternoon | Warm light and long shadows |

### Weather Moments

| Weather | Feeling |
|---|---|

- Balloons drifting in the sky.

- Sunlight shining through clouds.

## 12. Player Controls

The controls should be simple and forgiving.

### Basic Actions

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| Turn left | Plane gently moves left |
| Turn right | Plane gently moves right |
| Fly up | Plane rises slightly |
| Fly down | Plane lowers slightly |
| Speed up | Plane moves faster |
| Slow down | Plane moves slower |
| Take photo | Saves a landmark photo |
| Auto-help | Helps the plane fly safely |
| Land | Starts guided landing when near an airport |

### Control Feeling

- The airplane should move smoothly.
- The plane should never spin wildly.
- If the child stops touching controls, the plane should level out.
- If the child flies too low, the game should help.
- If the child misses the runway, the game should gently guide the plane back.

## 13. Takeoff Scenario

Takeoff should be simple and exciting.

### Takeoff Steps

1. The airplane waits at the start of the runway.
2. A friendly message says: "Ready to fly?"
3. The child holds the speed button.
4. Runway lights and lines move faster.
5. The airplane shakes gently.
6. A guide says: "Pull up gently."
7. The plane lifts off.
8. The airport becomes smaller below.
9. Clouds and the city appear ahead.

### Takeoff Feeling

- Fast enough to be exciting.
- Slow enough for young children to understand.
- Always successful in easy mode.

## 14. Cruise Flight Scenario

This is the main sightseeing part of the game.

### Cruise Goals

- Follow the general route.
- Enjoy dynamic weather conditions (clear sky, dense clouds, storms).
- See changing landscapes (mountain peaks, cities, oceans, and islands).
- Take photos of landmarks.
- Collect travel stamps by discovering landmarks.
- Enjoy the sky.

### Optional Route Activities

| Activity | Description |
|---|---|
| Weather Transitions | Fly through clear skies, dense cloud layers, and storm zones with realistic turbulence and caution systems |
| Photo Moment | Take a photo of a landmark |
| Follow the River | Fly along a visible river below |
| Rainbow Pass | Fly under or near a rainbow |
| Bird Watching | Watch birds fly safely nearby |

## 15. Landing Scenario

Landing should feel like an achievement, not a punishment.

### Landing Steps

1. The game announces: "The airport is nearby."
2. The runway appears far ahead.
3. A soft path or light trail guides the airplane.
4. The child slows down.
5. The plane lowers gently.
6. Runway lights become clearer.
7. The plane touches down.
8. The game celebrates the landing.

### Landing Results

Landing can have friendly ratings:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Great Landing | Smooth and centered |
| Nice Landing | Safe landing |
| Assisted Landing | The game helped, but the flight is complete |

No result should feel like failure for young children.

## 16. Progression and Rewards

Progression should reward exploration.

### Reward Types

| Reward | How to Earn |
|---|---|
| Airport Stamp | Land at a new airport |
| Landmark Photo | Take a photo during flight |
| Route Badge | Complete a route |
| Sky Color | Complete scenic flights |
| Plane Paint | Finish several flights |
| Travel Album Page | Discover all landmarks in a region |

### Travel Album
The album is a collection of memories.

Each page can show:

- Route name.
- Airport stamps.
- Landmark photos.
- Short facts.
- Date or flight count.
- A small map line.

### Unlock Flow

1. First Flight unlocks Hanoi to Da Nang.
2. Completing Hanoi to Da Nang unlocks Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City.
3. Completing two domestic routes unlocks Singapore.
4. Taking enough landmark photos unlocks Japan.
5. Completing several international flights unlocks the United States route.

## 17. User Interface Scenario

### Main Menu
The first screen should immediately show the flying theme.

Suggested elements:

- A friendly airplane.
- Moving clouds.
- Big "Fly Now" button.
- "Choose Route" button.
- "Travel Album" button.
- "My Airplane" button.

### Route Selection
The child chooses a route from illustrated cards.

Each card should show:

- Start city.
- Destination city.
- A small route line.
- Main landmark image.
- Difficulty icon.
- Flight time.

### In-Flight Display
The screen should stay clean.

Important display elements:

- Speed.
- Height.
- Small route progress.
- 4-direction Navigation Radar Compass (displays flight heading HDG and N/S/E/W coordinates).
- Camera button.
- Auto-help button.
- Landing button when available.

### End Screen
After landing, the game shows:

- Route completed.
- Airport stamp earned.
- Landmark photos.
- New unlocks.
- Suggested next flight.

## 18. Character and Plane Style

### Airplane Style
The airplane should look friendly and readable.

Possible plane styles:

- Small passenger airplane.
- Colorful propeller plane.
- Cute training aircraft.
- Special themed plane skins.

### Plane Personality
The airplane can feel cheerful through:

- Soft colors.
- Gentle animation.
- Happy sound effects.
- Optional expressive cockpit window.

### Plane Customization
Children can unlock:

- Paint colors.
- Stickers.
- Wing patterns.
- Trail effects.
- Pilot badge icons.

Customization should be cosmetic only.

## 19. Guidance and Safety

### Friendly Guidance
The game should guide the child with short messages:

- "Turn gently."
- "Fly through the clouds."
- "Look, a landmark!"
- "Take a photo!"
- "The airport is ahead."
- "Slow down for landing."

### Safety Rules

- The plane should not explode or crash dramatically.
- If the player flies too far away, the game gently turns the plane back.
- If the player gets too low, the game helps lift the plane.
- If landing is missed, the plane circles and tries again.
- Young children should always be able to finish a flight.

## 20. MVP Game Scenario

### MVP Route

1. Opens the game.
2. Presses "Fly Now."
3. Starts at Noi Bai Airport.

5. Flies over Hanoi.

### MVP Must Feel Complete
Even with only one route, the game should already feel like a full journey:

- Start.
- Takeoff.
- Discovery.
- Landmark.
- Landing.
- Reward.

## 24. Final Design Direction

Flight Adventure Kids should be a peaceful flying journey for children. The most important part is not technical realism, but the feeling of taking off, flying through a beautiful world, discovering real places, and landing safely.

The first version should focus on one polished route from Hanoi to Da Nang. Once that journey feels good, the game can grow by adding more airports, routes, landmarks, sky moments, and travel album rewards.
